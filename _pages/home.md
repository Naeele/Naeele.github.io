### Who am I? 👀

I am a postdoctoral researcher at the [University of Lübeck](https://www.uni-luebeck.de/en/), Germany, working at the Institute of IT Security, under the supervision of [Thomas Eisenbarth](https://www.its.uni-luebeck.de/en/staff/thomas-eisenbarth). I recently completed my <font color="green">P</font>hD in cybersecurity at the [Inria Centre at the University of Lille](https://www.inria.fr/en/inria-centre-university-lille), within the [CRIStAL laboratory](https://cristal.univ-lille.fr/?rubrique9), under the superv<font color="green">i</font>sion of [Clémentine Maurice](https://cmaurice.fr/).

My current research interests lie at the intersection of cybersecurity and AI. I want to explore how AI, particularly Large Language Models (LLMs) and agentic AI, can be used for security-related tasks. I am also interested in understanding how the growing adoption of AI by software developers affects software engineering practices, particularly in cybersecurity. I would love to explore these questions through interdisciplinary collaborations with researchers in fields such as sociology.

If you would like to discuss ideas, collaborate, or just chat, feel free to send me an email 😉
